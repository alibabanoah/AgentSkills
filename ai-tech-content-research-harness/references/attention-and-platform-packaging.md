# Attention And Platform Packaging

Use this reference to package verified AI tech content for self-media platforms.

## Principle

Make the content attractive by sharpening framing, not by distorting facts. The content can be dramatic, but the facts must remain honest.

Before packaging a full draft, read `secondary-creation-stylebook.md` to choose the voice and intensity. Packaging should amplify the chosen style rather than create a separate tone.

## Title formulas

Use:

- `[Big name] just revealed the next [industry/career] signal`
- `This AI case is not about tools. It is about [deeper mechanism]`
- `The real danger is not AI replacing you, but [specific hidden loss]`
- `A [ordinary person] used AI to break the old [industry] barrier`
- `Why [new technology] may change how [audience] learn/work/create`
- `The AI opportunity most people are looking at backwards`

## Cover text formulas

Keep cover text short:

- "AI is removing the ladder"
- "The new job is not prompt engineer"
- "A 3000 yuan signal"
- "Education is becoming software"
- "The founder signal everyone missed"
- "Taste beats prompts"

## Opening hooks

Use one of:

- surprising fact
- direct challenge
- famous-person signal
- ordinary-person consequence
- before/after comparison
- contradiction between public narrative and real mechanism

Examples:

```text
Most people think this is an AI education tool. I think it is a signal that courses are becoming software.
```

```text
The most dangerous thing AI replaces may not be your current job, but the beginner tasks that used to train you.
```

## Platform defaults

WeChat public account:

- more complete argument
- evidence chain
- restrained but strong headline
- structure: case -> mechanism -> impact -> method -> watch point

Xiaohongshu:

- sharper title
- shorter paragraphs
- more personal relevance
- practical takeaways
- avoid overly academic framing

Short video:

- first 3 seconds: conflict or surprise
- 30-90 seconds: one case, one mechanism, one conclusion
- use spoken rhythm and simple transitions

Bilibili/long video:

- deeper background
- timeline and sources
- stronger narrative arc
- explain technology in plain language

## Ethical exaggeration

Allowed:

- "may reshape"
- "a signal worth watching"
- "the hidden risk"
- "ordinary people should pay attention"
- "this is not just X, it is Y"

Avoid:

- "will definitely"
- "everyone will be replaced"
- "the truth nobody dares say" unless genuinely supported
- "confirmed" for unconfirmed reports
- turning one case into universal proof

## Final packaging checklist

Before final output, check:

- Is the hook strong?
- Is the core claim verified?
- Is the exaggeration in the framing rather than the facts?
- Does the audience know why it matters to them?
- Is there a practical takeaway?
- Are uncertainty and source limits handled?
