# Influencer And Investor Tracking

Use this reference when a topic depends on tech founders, investors, company leaders, or public narratives from famous people.

## Tracking targets

Priority figures and entities:

- Elon Musk: Tesla, SpaceX, Starlink, Neuralink, xAI, Optimus, Robotaxi, autonomous driving, Mars, energy, AI safety.
- Sam Altman: OpenAI, AGI, compute, AI agents, education, jobs, universal basic income, regulation, startups.
- Jensen Huang: Nvidia, AI chips, data centers, robotics, sovereign AI, accelerated computing.
- Mark Zuckerberg: Meta AI, open-source models, AR/VR, AI social platforms, creator tools.
- Jeff Bezos, Satya Nadella, Sundar Pichai, Demis Hassabis, Dario Amodei, Ilya Sutskever, Fei-Fei Li, Andrew Ng, Kai-Fu Lee.
- Investors and funds: a16z, Sequoia, YC, Khosla, Founders Fund, ARK, Benchmark, Lightspeed, Coatue, major AI infrastructure investors.

## Source priority

Prefer:

1. Original post, full interview, conference transcript, earnings call, keynote, shareholder letter, or official blog.
2. Reputable media report with named reporter and clear sourcing.
3. High-quality clipped video or translated summary only after checking the original context.
4. Social interpretation only as a content lead.

## Narrative extraction

For each public statement, extract:

- what was actually said
- what was implied
- what was not said but people are interpreting
- why the statement matters now
- what company, product, industry, or career trend it affects
- whether the statement is a prediction, ambition, current fact, or marketing claim

## Founder narrative patterns

Common reusable angles:

- "One sentence reveals the next battlefield."
- "The founder is not selling a product; he is building infrastructure."
- "The product launch is small, but the strategic signal is large."
- "The controversy matters because it exposes who pays the cost of AI progress."
- "Investors are moving before ordinary people understand the mechanism."

## Investor signal analysis

When tracking investors:

- identify where money is flowing: chips, data centers, agents, robotics, AI search, enterprise workflow, education, biotech, defense, energy.
- compare public statements with portfolio moves and funding rounds.
- separate conviction from marketing.
- ask what old job, industry, or workflow the investment thesis assumes will change.

## Quote discipline

- Quote only if the exact wording is verified from the original or reputable transcript.
- If paraphrasing, say "the argument is" rather than presenting it as a quote.
- Do not intensify claims such as "replace all humans", "death of education", or "revive the dead" unless the source uses those words and context supports it.
