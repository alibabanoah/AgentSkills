# Topic Discovery

Use this reference to discover, score, and select AI technology self-media topics.

## Topic sources

Use multiple routes:

- frontier signal: new model, product, paper, benchmark, open-source project, hardware, robotics, AI video, AI education tool.
- people signal: founder statement, investor thesis, tech leader interview, controversial quote, public debate.
- company signal: launch, earnings call, acquisition, funding, layoffs, hiring, product pivot, regulation.
- social signal: viral demo, creator case, comment-section pain, platform trend, meme, repeated audience question.
- structural signal: job-market change, education reform, capital flow, AI replacing workflows, new skill demand.

## Topic scoring

Score a lead from 1-5 on:

- freshness: is it recent or newly relevant?
- authority: does it have strong sources?
- conflict: does it contain tension or reversal?
- audience relevance: does it affect ordinary people, careers, learning, money, parenting, creators, or business?
- mechanism depth: can it explain a deeper pattern?
- actionability: can the audience do, think, avoid, or learn something?
- risk: are facts uncertain, defamatory, or overly speculative?

Prioritize topics with high freshness, authority, conflict, and audience relevance. Reject or delay topics with high risk and weak evidence.

## Reusable topic formulas

Use these formulas:

```text
Big name said X -> the hidden meaning is Y -> ordinary people should watch Z
```

```text
Small creator did X with AI -> old production barrier collapsed -> the new scarce ability is Y
```

```text
Institution launched AI education tool -> learning process changes -> students/parents/teachers should rethink X
```

```text
AI creates new job category -> the real skill is not tool use but business/context judgment
```

```text
Capital leaves old system -> money flows to AI infrastructure -> social opportunity/risk changes
```

## Content buckets

Maintain these recurring buckets:

- AI education and learning revolution
- AI career shock and new roles
- AI tools and workflow transformation
- founder/investor frontier predictions
- creative individual and AI-native production
- robotics, autonomous driving, brain-computer interface, space, chips, data centers
- China vs global AI ecosystem
- ethical tension: inequality, privacy, job entry barriers, human value

## Selection discipline

Before writing, answer:

- What is new?
- What is verified?
- What is the conflict?
- Why should an ordinary person care?
- What is the deeper mechanism?
- What can be said responsibly?
- What must not be overstated?
