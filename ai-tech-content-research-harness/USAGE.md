# AI Tech Content Research Harness Usage

This skill helps research AI technology trends, verify sources, discover self-media topics, and transform reliable information into compelling secondary content.

## What It Does

- Monitor AI technology signals from labs, founders, investors, media, papers, open-source projects, education, jobs, and creator communities.
- Evaluate whether a signal is worth turning into a topic.
- Verify claims before using them in public content.
- Convert raw information into strong content angles.
- Choose a secondary-creation style such as sharp commentary, calm analysis, ordinary-person translation, crisis warning, opportunity playbook, founder-signal decoding, case-story narrative, or methodology teardown.
- Package output for WeChat, Xiaohongshu, short video, Bilibili, newsletters, or topic memos.

## How To Call It

Use the skill by name:

```text
Use $ai-tech-content-research-harness to find today's AI technology topics worth writing.
```

Natural request examples:

```text
Use the AI tech content research skill to find AI topics suitable for short videos today.
```

```text
Use ai-tech-content-research-harness to analyze Elon Musk's latest statement and decide whether it can become a short video.
```

```text
Update the AI tech content research skill with new sources or secondary-creation styles.
```

## Important Guardrails

- Use social media as leads, not proof.
- Verify important claims through original sources, papers, company releases, public reports, or reputable named reporting.
- Do not invent quotes, statistics, events, companies, papers, or product capabilities.
- Moderate exaggeration is allowed only in framing, contrast, metaphor, and audience stakes.
- Do not exaggerate facts, numbers, causality, certainty, or legal/ethical accusations.
