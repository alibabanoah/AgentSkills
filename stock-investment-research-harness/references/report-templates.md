## Language guardrails

Prefer phrases equivalent to:

- "research candidate"
- "requires review"
- "risk level upgraded/downgraded"
- "more attractive under these assumptions"
- "if X happens, the view should be updated"

Avoid phrases equivalent to:

- "must buy"
- "guaranteed profit"
- "sell immediately"
- "certain to rise"
- "inside information"
- "risk-free"
